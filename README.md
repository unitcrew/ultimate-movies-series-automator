# Ultimate Movies/Series Playlists for Stremio

Ranked movie and series playlists that show up as rows on Stremio's home screen, built from IMDb and Letterboxd data, with filters by country, decade, genre, awards, streaming service and what's in cinemas now.

### 👉 [Open the install page](https://unitcrew.github.io/ultimate-movies-series-automator/)

---

## Two ways to use it

### ① Just add the playlists to Stremio (nothing to install)
1. Have [Stremio](https://www.stremio.com/downloads) installed, or use [Stremio Web](https://web.stremio.com/).
2. Open the [install page](https://unitcrew.github.io/ultimate-movies-series-automator/#stremio) and press **Install in Stremio** on each add-on.

55+ ready-made playlists, including:
- **Awards:** Best Oscar Winners, Best Emmy Winners (Films / Series), Most Awarded Films and Series of All Time, with dropdowns by category and ceremony year
- **All-time and recent:** Best Movies / Series of All Time, Best Recent Films and Series, Forgotten Classics, Underseen Films, Best Short Films
- **World cinema:** Brazil, Latin America, Caribbean, Africa, the Middle East, Asia, Japan (live action and anime), Korea, Bollywood, Oceania, the Soviet Union and the Eastern Bloc, the rarest film nations
- **Right now:** what's popular, and the best films showing in São Paulo cinemas and Brazilian festivals
- **Streaming:** the best of Netflix and HBO Max

The lists update by themselves every time they're published, so you don't need to reinstall.

### ② Run your own copy (make your own playlists)
The **Playlist Automator** is a small Windows app that runs on your PC. It builds playlists with your own filters (rating, votes, Letterboxd score, country, language, decade, runtime, genre, type, awards, streaming service, hide what you've seen…) and publishes them to your own Stremio.

1. **[Download the app (.zip)](https://github.com/unitcrew/ultimate-movies-series-automator/releases/latest)**
2. Unzip it anywhere, for example in Documents.
3. Double-click **`Start.bat`**. The first time, it installs Node.js for you (free) and opens the app in your browser.
4. Build your playlists and press **Install in Stremio**.

Your copy is completely separate: its own add-ons, settings and data. The app tells you when a new version is out and updates itself with one click.
