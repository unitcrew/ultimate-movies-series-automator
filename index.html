② Run your own copy

The same app that makes these playlists, on your PC: your own filters, your own watched list, your own Stremio add-ons.

Download the app (Windows, 0.4 MB)
  1. Unzip the file anywhere (right-click → Extract All).
  2. Open the Playlist Automator folder and double-click Start.bat. The first time it checks your PC and installs Node.js if needed (Windows asks permission — accept).
  3. Your browser opens the app. Press Download the IMDb index at the top (about 10 minutes, once). Got a data pack from a friend? Import it instead (the app's Share section, at the bottom) and skip most of the waiting.
  4. It starts with these playlists as examples. Edit them or make your own, then press Install next to one to add it to Stremio.

Your copy is completely yours: its own settings, data and add-ons, which never replace or change the ones on this page. When a newer version comes out, the app shows an Update button. Mac/Linux: install Node.js 18+, then run node src/server.js in the folder.
